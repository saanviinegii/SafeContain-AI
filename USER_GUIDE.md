## 1. Operating SafeContain AI

SafeContain AI organizes cyber containment operations into a streamlined 5-step operational workflow:

### Step 1: Ingest & Triage Incidents
- Navigate to **SOC Operations** or **Topology Explorer**.
- Inspect incoming telemetry alerts. Identify red-highlighted compromised endpoints or external threat actors.

### Step 2: Explore Attack Paths & Uncertainty
- Navigate to **Attack Paths** to view all multi-hop lateral routes to crown jewels.
- Open **Evidence Uncertainty** to see whether key edges are `CONFIRMED` or `SUSPECTED`. Run sensitivity scenarios to test if confirming an unverified link alters priorities.

### Step 3: Run Tri-Level Optimization
- Navigate to **Strategy Comparator**.
- Review the comparison table:
  - Observe how Strategy 1 (Baseline) causes severe disruption to shared services.
  - Observe how Strategy 3 (CP-SAT) protects mission-critical citizen workflows while reducing systemic risk under budget.

### Step 4: Execute What-If Simulation
- Navigate to **Simulation Sandbox**.
- Click **Re-run What-If Simulation**.
- Review the before/after risk delta and check **Residual Risk Inspector** to verify which secondary attack routes were successfully severed.

### Step 5: Governance Review & Audit
- Navigate to **Human Approval Center**.
- Review decision justification in **Decision Explainer**.
- Transition status from `PENDING_APPROVAL` to `APPROVED`.
- Inspect the **Cryptographic Audit Ledger** to verify that your approval was permanently anchored in a SHA-256 block.
