1 Core Scientific Foundations & Research Contributions

SafeContain AI bridges the critical operational gap between Network Attack Graphs (e.g., MulVAL, NetSPA), Automated SOAR Playbooks, and Business Continuity Management. Traditional tools suffer from three foundational flaws:

  • The Containment Blindspot: SOAR playbooks blindly sever perimeter gateways or isolate servers, causing massive unintended collateral damage to revenue and citizen services.
  • The Uncertainty Fallacy: Classic attack graph algorithms assume 100% forensic telemetry certainty, while real-world forensic observations decay exponentially and carry epistemic ambiguity.
  • The Containment Assumption Fallacy: Blocking one known attack path is naively celebrated as complete resolution, leaving secondary or tertiary lateral backdoor routes undetected and open.

4. Attack Paths View: ATTACK_PATHS
R&D Gap Solved: Adversaries traverse multi-hop lateral routes across trusted permissions. Single-point vulnerability scans fail to discover these attack chains.
Scientific Method: Bounded Depth-First Search traversal algorithm ($cutoff = 7$) mapping simple directed paths from compromised entry nodes to crown jewels. Identifies topological choke points (nodes with highest path-intersection centrality).
Backend Module: backend/app/graph/algorithms.py (discover_attack_paths())
Judge Pitch: "Attack Paths calculates every viable multi-hop lateral traversal route to critical databases and reveals the common choke points where an attacker can be severed."
5. Evidence Uncertainty View: EVIDENCE_UNCERTAINTY
R&D Gap Solved: The Uncertainty Fallacy: Treating unverified or heuristic telemetry as confirmed facts leads to catastrophic false positives or missed breaches.
Scientific Method: 5-State Epistemic Evidence Framework (CONFIRMED, SUSPECTED, UNKNOWN, DISPUTED, STALE) with Bayesian temporal confidence decay:
c(t) = c_0 · e-λ t   |   λ = 0.05 / day
Coupled with Sensitivity Drift Analysis to test if perturbing suspected links flips the containment recommendation.
Backend Module: backend/app/evidence/engine.py (EvidenceEngine)
Judge Pitch: "SafeContain AI introduces uncertainty-aware containment: decaying confidence over time and attaching explicit uncertainty penalties to suspected links rather than assuming 100% telemetry certainty."
6. Asset Intelligence View: ASSET_INTELLIGENCE

Backend Module: backend/app/graph/engine.py (get_database_statistics())

Backend Module: backend/app/audit/ledger.py (ChainedAuditLedger)
Judge Pitch: "Every recommendation, simulation, and approval is anchored in a tamper-evident SHA-256 cryptographic audit chain backed by an EVM smart contract, making unauthorized log modification impossible."
